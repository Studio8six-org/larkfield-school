## Before you publish
The Pexels licence does not guarantee model releases. Many of these photos show real, identifiable people, including children. The Studio8six brief says no identifiable real children without written consent, so for a public demo swap child photos for facilities-only shots, photos where faces are not identifiable, or generated images.
